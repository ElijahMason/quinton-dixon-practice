Management 03

Management is the calendar, attention to money, and the decision list.

Calendar 03

The calendar shows what is due and who owns it.

A weekly routine puts the due work on one sheet and names the owner of each item.

Money 03

Attention to money keeps three questions in view.

What is committed. What is unpaid. What is waiting on a decision. Amounts stay in your books.

Decisions 03

Open choices sit on a list with a date.

Each item names the choice and the day it has to be made.
