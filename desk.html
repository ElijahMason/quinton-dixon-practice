Management is the calendar, attention to money, and the decision list.

Calendar

The weekly routine lists what is due and who owns it.

Money

Attention to money keeps three questions in view. What is committed. What is unpaid. What is waiting on a decision. Amounts stay in your books.

Decisions

Open choices sit on a list. Each one names the choice and the day it has to be made.
